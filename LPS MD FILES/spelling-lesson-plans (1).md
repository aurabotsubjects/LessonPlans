# Year 5 Spelling Programme — Mini Lesson Plans (Terms 1–4)

_Personal planning record only — not linked to or synced with the Spelling app. Built directly from the app's own Lesson Planner engine: the Monday/Tuesday/Wednesday activity for any given week is fixed by the app's built-in weekly rotation through its 21-activity library; Thursday is always "Stop, Spell, Smell"; Friday is always the dictated weekly test. The actual word list each week is adaptive per student (any word a student gets wrong on Friday rolls into the following week), so the word lists shown below are a realistic **worked example** — generated by running the app's own list-building algorithm for a new Year 5 student who masters every word on the first attempt. In real use, a class's lists will vary student-by-student depending on test results._

_Note on the sample word list: the Year 5 bank in the app has 95 words. At 10 new words a week with perfect mastery, a student works through the entire bank by Week 10 — after that, the app would prompt moving them up to Year 6. From Term 2 onward, the sample list below is left blank for that reason; in a real class, most students are still cycling through review + new words well beyond Week 10, so the day-by-day activity plan (which does not depend on the specific words) is what carries the real long-term value here._

### Week 1

**This week's word list (sample, 10 words):** `accident`, `actually`, `adventure`, `afraid`, `against`, `August`, `beautiful`, `bicycle`, `bought`, `breath`

#### Monday

**Learning Intention:** We are learning to recognise the correct letter sequence of our spelling words within a field of distractor letters.

**Success Criteria:** I can spot my whole spelling word, in order, hidden across, down, or diagonally.

**Activity: 🔎 Word Search Hunt** _(Individual · ~20 min)_

Hunt for this week's spelling words hidden in a letter grid — words can run across, down, or diagonally.

_You'll need:_ Printed or on-screen word search · Pencil or highlighter
- **2 min** — Look at the word list and read each word aloud before you start hunting.
- **15 min** — Circle or highlight every word as you find it in the grid.
- **3 min** — Tell a partner which word was trickiest to spot and why.

#### Tuesday

**Learning Intention:** We are learning to reconstruct a spelling word when its letters are presented out of order.

**Success Criteria:** I can unscramble a mixed-up word back into its correct spelling, quickly and confidently.

**Activity: 🌀 Speedy Scramble Swap** _(Pairs · ~20 min)_

Partners take turns unscrambling each other's mixed-up spelling words as fast as they can.

_You'll need:_ Whiteboard or paper · Timer
- **3 min** — Partner A reads out the scrambled letters for word 1 — Partner B unscrambles and spells it aloud.
- **12 min** — Take turns through the whole list, swapping roles each word.
- **5 min** — Whoever unscrambled the most words correctly picks the next warm-up game!

#### Wednesday

**Learning Intention:** We are learning to build muscle memory for tricky spelling patterns through repeated, multisensory writing.

**Success Criteria:** I can write each word correctly three times, saying each letter as I go.

**Activity: 🌈 Rainbow Write** _(Individual · ~20 min)_

Write each spelling word three times, using a different colour pencil each time, tracing over the same letters.

_You'll need:_ 3+ coloured pencils or markers · Plain paper
- **2 min** — Choose your 3 colours for today.
- **15 min** — Write each word once in each colour, saying each letter as you write it.
- **3 min** — Pick your favourite rainbow word and show a partner.

#### Thursday

**Learning Intention:** We are learning to deepen our memory of a spelling word by connecting its spelling, its meaning, and its use in a sentence.

**Success Criteria:** I can write my word correctly three times, draw a picture that shows its meaning, and write a sentence that matches my picture.

**Activity: 🛑🔤👃 Stop, Spell, Smell** _(Individual · ~20 min)_

Choose 5 of this week's words. For each one: write it correctly 3 times, draw a picture showing what it means, then write a sentence using the word that matches the picture.

_You'll need:_ Worksheet or notebook · Pencil · Colouring pencils/markers

#### Friday

**Learning Intention:** We are learning to spell this week's words correctly and independently, without support.

**Success Criteria:** I can write each dictated word with the correct spelling, unaided.

**Activity: 📝 Friday Spelling Test** _(Individual · teacher-administered)_

Teacher reads each word aloud (in a sentence if helpful), student writes it, then the teacher marks each word ✓ correct or ✗ incorrect. Any word marked incorrect automatically reappears on next week's list for review; mastered words are replaced with new words from the Year 5 bank.

### Week 2

**This week's word list (sample, 10 words):** `breathe`, `business`, `chocolate`, `colour`, `complete`, `consider`, `continue`, `country`, `cousin`, `describe`

#### Monday

**Learning Intention:** We are learning to build up a word sound-by-sound and letter-by-letter, strengthening our sense of letter order.

**Success Criteria:** I can build a correct letter pyramid for every word on my list.

**Activity: 🔺 Pyramid Words** _(Individual · ~20 min)_

Build each spelling word one letter at a time, like a growing staircase, until the whole word is spelled out.

_You'll need:_ Paper · Pencil
- **2 min** — Teacher shows an example pyramid on the board (e.g. b / be / bec / beca...).
- **15 min** — Build a pyramid for every word on your list.
- **3 min** — Trade books with a neighbour and check each other's pyramids.

#### Tuesday

**Learning Intention:** We are learning to spell words aloud fluently and automatically, one letter at a time, under light time pressure.

**Success Criteria:** I can say my share of the letters correctly without hesitating, and say the whole word at the end.

**Activity: 🎾 Word Tennis** _(Pairs · ~20 min)_

Partners "volley" a word back and forth, each saying the next letter — miss a letter and you lose the point!

_You'll need:_ Just your voices!
- **2 min** — Decide who serves first for word 1.
- **15 min** — Rally through the whole list — one letter each, back and forth, then say the whole word together at the end.
- **3 min** — Total up the points and celebrate the winner.

#### Wednesday

**Learning Intention:** We are learning to recall and say a spelling word correctly from memory, under a small amount of performance pressure.

**Success Criteria:** I can spell my word aloud, letter by letter, without looking at the list.

**Activity: 🎤 Spelling Bee Countdown** _(Team · ~20 min)_

Teams take turns spelling words aloud — get it right and stay in, get it wrong and sit down. Last team standing wins!

_You'll need:_ Word list (teacher calls words) · Nothing else needed
- **3 min** — Split into teams of 3–4 and line up.
- **14 min** — Teacher calls a word, first player in line spells it aloud. Correct = go to the back of the line. Wrong = sit down.
- **3 min** — Last team with players standing wins the round!

#### Thursday

**Learning Intention:** We are learning to deepen our memory of a spelling word by connecting its spelling, its meaning, and its use in a sentence.

**Success Criteria:** I can write my word correctly three times, draw a picture that shows its meaning, and write a sentence that matches my picture.

### Week 3

**This week's word list (sample, 10 words):** `difficult`, `disappeared`, `divide`, `easy`, `equal`, `everybody`, `everywhere`, `excited`, `exercise`, `experience`

#### Monday

**Learning Intention:** We are learning to write a spelling word correctly and legibly at speed.

**Success Criteria:** I can run up, write my word correctly first try, and tag my teammate.

**Activity: 🌍 Around the World Relay** _(Team · ~20 min)_

A running relay — race to the board, write the next word correctly, then tag your teammate.

_You'll need:_ Whiteboard/chalkboard or paper on a wall · Markers
- **3 min** — Split into even teams and line up single-file.
- **14 min** — On "go", the first runner races up, writes word 1 correctly, runs back and tags the next player who writes word 2, and so on.
- **3 min** — Check every team's words together — first fully-correct list wins.

#### Tuesday

**Learning Intention:** We are learning to use knowledge of spelling patterns to predict letters that are missing from a word.

**Success Criteria:** I can fill every gap in a word with the correct missing letter(s).

**Activity: 🕳️ Mystery Missing Letters** _(Individual · ~20 min)_

Some letters have vanished from each spelling word — figure out which ones and fill in the gaps.

_You'll need:_ Worksheet or notebook · Pencil
- **2 min** — Look at the first mystery word and predict the missing letters.
- **15 min** — Fill in every missing letter for all the words on your list.
- **3 min** — Swap with a partner and check each other's answers.

#### Wednesday

**Learning Intention:** We are learning to spell a word correctly when we hear it inside a full sentence, rather than in isolation.

**Success Criteria:** I can listen for my word inside a sentence and write it with the correct spelling.

**Activity: ✍️ Partner Dictation Challenge** _(Pairs · ~20 min)_

One partner reads a word inside a sentence, the other writes it down — then swap and check together.

_You'll need:_ Paper · Pencil
- **2 min** — Decide who dictates first.
- **14 min** — Dictator says a sentence containing the spelling word; writer writes just the word. Swap roles halfway through the list.
- **4 min** — Compare your writing to the real spelling and fix any mistakes together.

**Learning Intention:** We are learning to deepen our memory of a spelling word by connecting its spelling, its meaning, and its use in a sentence.

### Week 4

**This week's word list (sample, 10 words):** `experiment`, `extreme`, `February`, `finally`, `follow`, `forty`, `frightened`, `guard`, `happened`, `height`

#### Monday

**Learning Intention:** We are learning to break spelling words into syllables to help us spell longer or trickier words.

**Success Criteria:** I can clap and count the syllables in each word and agree on the total with my partner.

**Activity: 👏 Syllable Clap Count** _(Pairs · ~20 min)_

Clap out the syllables in each spelling word, then work out how many "chunks" it breaks into.

_You'll need:_ Just your hands!
- **3 min** — Warm up by clapping your own name.
- **14 min** — Take turns saying and clapping each spelling word, then agree on the syllable count and write it down.
- **3 min** — Find the word on your list with the most syllables — can you beat it next week?

#### Tuesday

**Learning Intention:** We are learning to notice patterns across our spelling words (e.g. syllables, tricky letter strings, silent letters) and use them to group words.

**Success Criteria:** I can sort every word into a group using a spelling pattern I chose myself.

**Activity: 🗂️ Word Sort Categories** _(Pairs · ~20 min)_

Sort this week's words into your own categories — by syllables, tricky letter patterns, or silent letters.

_You'll need:_ Small cards or sticky notes with words written on them · Table or floor space
- **3 min** — Write each word onto its own card.
- **14 min** — Choose a sorting rule together (e.g. number of syllables) and sort every word into groups.
- **3 min** — Explain your sorting rule to another pair.

#### Wednesday

**Learning Intention:** We are learning to reinforce the correct letter sequence of a word through a creative, visual activity.

**Success Criteria:** I can draw and colour each word so that every letter is in the correct order.

**Activity: 🎨 Bubble Letter Word Art** _(Individual · ~20 min)_

Draw each spelling word in big bubble or graffiti letters and decorate it.

_You'll need:_ Plain paper · Pencil, then colouring pencils/markers
- **2 min** — Look at an example bubble-letter word on the board.
- **15 min** — Draw and colour bubble letters for as many words as you can.
- **3 min** — Show off your favourite word-art creation to the class.

**Learning Intention:** We are learning to deepen our memory of a spelling word by connecting its spelling, its meaning, and its use in a sentence.

### Week 5

**This week's word list (sample, 10 words):** `history`, `holidays`, `hospital`, `imagine`, `increase`, `instead`, `interest`, `interesting`, `island`, `January`

#### Monday

**Learning Intention:** We are learning to recall spelling words correctly on demand, in a low-pressure game format.

**Success Criteria:** I can spell the word correctly before I take my turn to move.

**Activity: 🐍 Spelling Snakes & Ladders** _(Team · ~20 min)_

Play snakes and ladders — but you can only move if you spell a word correctly first!

_You'll need:_ Snakes & ladders board (drawn or printed) · Dice · Counters
- **2 min** — Set up the board and counters in groups of 3–4.
- **15 min** — Roll the dice — before moving, spell the next word on your list aloud. Get it right to move, wrong to stay put.
- **3 min** — First to reach the end (or whoever's furthest when time is up) wins.

#### Tuesday

**Learning Intention:** We are learning to use our spelling words correctly and meaningfully within a sentence of our own.

**Success Criteria:** I can add a sentence to our class story that uses my spelling word correctly.

**Activity: 📖 Silly Sentence Story Chain** _(Team · ~20 min)_

Build one giant silly class story together, where each sentence must use the next spelling word.

_You'll need:_ Paper or shared doc to record the story
- **3 min** — Sit in a circle with your team and decide on a story character.
- **14 min** — Take turns adding one sentence to the story, each one using a spelling word correctly.
- **3 min** — Read your finished silly story aloud to another team.

#### Wednesday

**Learning Intention:** We are learning to recall and say a spelling word correctly from memory in a fun, active game.

**Success Criteria:** I can spell my word aloud correctly when I catch the beanbag.

**Activity: 🎯 Beanbag Toss Spelling** _(Team · ~20 min)_

Toss a beanbag or soft ball — whoever catches it has to spell the next word out loud.

_You'll need:_ Beanbag or soft ball · Open space
- **2 min** — Stand in a circle with your team.
- **15 min** — Toss the beanbag — catcher spells the next word aloud. Correct = toss again for the next word; incorrect = get a hint and try once more.
- **3 min** — Celebrate every word your team got through together.

#### Monday

**Learning Intention:** We are learning that understanding what a word means helps us remember how to spell it.

**Success Criteria:** I can guess a sensible definition for each word, then check it against the real meaning.

**Activity: 🕵️ Definition Detective** _(Individual · ~20 min)_

Before checking a dictionary, guess what each spelling word means — then check how close you were.

_You'll need:_ Notebook · Dictionary or teacher's help
- **2 min** — Pick the first word and think about where you've seen or heard it before.
- **15 min** — Write your own guessed definition for each word, then check the real meaning.
- **3 min** — Share the definition that surprised you most.

#### Tuesday

**Learning Intention:** We are learning to match a spelling word to its meaning or use in context.

**Success Criteria:** I can identify the correct word on my card when a clue or definition is read aloud.

**Activity: 🎱 Word Bingo Blast** _(Team · ~20 min)_

Fill a bingo card with this week's words — teacher reads a definition or sentence, you mark the matching word.

_You'll need:_ Bingo card with words written in a grid · Counters or pencil to mark squares
- **3 min** — Write your spelling words into a 3x3 or 4x4 grid in any order.
- **14 min** — Teacher reads a clue for a word — if it's on your card, mark it. First to a full line calls "Bingo!"
- **3 min** — Play again with a fresh grid if there's time.

#### Wednesday

**Learning Intention:** We are learning to reinforce spelling through large-scale kinesthetic movement rather than only pen and paper.

**Success Criteria:** I can air-write my word clearly enough for my partner to guess it correctly.

**Activity: 💫 Air Writing Wiggle** _(Pairs · ~20 min)_

"Write" a spelling word in the air with your finger, elbow, or nose — your partner has to guess it!

_You'll need:_ Just your body — no equipment needed
- **2 min** — Practise writing one letter big and slow in the air.
- **15 min** — Take turns air-writing each word while your partner guesses, then check the spelling together.
- **3 min** — Try writing your favourite word with your elbow for a giggle.

#### Monday

**Learning Intention:** We are learning to write spelling words correctly and legibly while moving at pace.

**Success Criteria:** I can chalk my word correctly before tagging the next teammate.

**Activity: 🖍️ Chalk & Talk Relay** _(Team · ~20 min)_

An outdoor (or hallway) relay where teams race to chalk out each spelling word correctly.

_You'll need:_ Chalk (or paper strips indoors) · Open path or hallway
- **3 min** — Split into teams and line up at the start line.
- **14 min** — One at a time, race up and chalk the next word correctly before running back to tag the next teammate.
- **3 min** — Walk along and check every team's words together.

#### Tuesday

**Learning Intention:** We are learning to reconstruct the correct letter order of a word as quickly as possible.

**Success Criteria:** I can flip and arrange my letter cards into the correct spelling before my partner does.

**Activity: 🔄 Flip & Spell Showdown** _(Pairs · ~20 min)_

Each player writes a spelling word's letters onto flip cards, shuffles them face-down, then races to flip them over in the correct order — whoever spells it correctly first wins the point!

_You'll need:_ Blank cards or small paper squares — one per letter · A pen each
- **3 min** — Both players write the letters of word 1 onto their own set of cards, then shuffle and lay them face-down.
- **14 min** — On "go", both players flip and rearrange their cards into the correct spelling order. First to spell it correctly wins the point — then reshuffle for the next word.
- **3 min** — Total up the points and celebrate the round's champion speller.

#### Wednesday

**Learning Intention:** We are learning that understanding the individual letters inside a word can help us see spelling patterns within other, smaller words.

**Success Criteria:** I can find smaller real words hidden inside a spelling word, using only its letters.

**Activity: 💰 Letter Loot Challenge** _(Team · ~20 min)_

Race a 30-second clock to spell as many smaller words as you can using only the letters from one spelling word. Score points for every letter you use — and grab a +2 bonus if you use every single letter!

_You'll need:_ Paper & pencil (or call out words aloud) · 30-second timer — built in below!
- **3 min** — Explain the scoring: each word you find scores points equal to how many letters it uses. Use up every letter from the original word across your words to earn a +2 bonus.
- **14 min** — Run a 30-second round for each spelling word — hunt for smaller words hiding inside it, then use the score checker below to total your points.
- **3 min** — Add up every round's score to crown the class Letter Loot Champion.
